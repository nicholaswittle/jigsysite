The style

Why Old Forge

Not New York. Not Chicago. Rectangular steel-pan pizza cut into squares — light crust, crisp bottom, no flop. Enola’s been eating it this way since 1999.

Red or white

Pick a base

Red is marinara and cheese. White skips the sauce — cheese between the crusts, single or double. No “supreme.”

By the tray

Not a pie

We bake rectangular in a seasoned steel pan, then load any of fifteen toppings. That’s how Old Forge has always done it.

3 · 6 · 12

Counted in cuts

Square “cuts,” not slices. Grab 3, split a 6, or feed the table with a 12. Corners go first.

Ordering

Order ahead

Order online for pickup, or call it in the old way. Same kitchen, same trays — your ticket lands on the staff console either way.

Before you call

FAQ

Phone-order & dine-in

How do I order?

Call (717) 732-7708. We’re phone-order and dine-in — no app, no online cart. Pick a tray (red or white, 3 / 6 / 12 cuts), add wings or a salad, and we’ll have it hot for pickup or the table.

Do you have a patio?

Yes — outdoor seating when the weather’s good. Come sit down, or call it in and grab it to go.

What’s downstairs?

A TV lounge for the game, skill games, and space for private parties. Ask when you call if you’re planning a group.

Do you cater?

Yes — Catering by Jigsy’s. Call the shop and we’ll walk you through trays, wings, and party spreads.
